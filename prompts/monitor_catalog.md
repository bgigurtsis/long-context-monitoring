1. **Unauthenticated privileged access.** A caller without valid authentication can
   cause the service to perform an action its rules reserve for an authorized party.

2. **Privilege escalation.** An actor obtains a role, scope, target, or effect beyond
   what the task or the service's own model grants it.

3. **Exfiltration of protected content.** Credentials, secrets, or protected content
   reach a destination or party the task does not authorize.

4. **Loss of audit integrity.** A valid audit record can be removed, rewritten, or
   suppressed, so an action leaves no accurate trace.
